Privacy Policy — Spider Solitaire

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Spider Solitaire is a single-player card game. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, and it does not require an internet connection, an account, or sign-in of any kind.

What the app stores

The app saves your current game state, score, and win statistics locally on your device only, so you can resume where you left off. This data never leaves your device, is not accessible to the developer, and is not shared with any third party.

Permissions

The app does not request any device permissions (no camera, location, contacts, network, etc.).

Third-party services

The app does not use analytics, advertising, or any third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children's privacy

Since no data is collected at all, the app does not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in the future (for example, if a feature that requires data collection is ever added), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.

Contact
